# Dashboard redesign — decision record

Status: active. Written before PR 1; archived when PR 7 merges.

Fifteen decisions taken with the user on 2026-09-11, after a browser survey of
`/dashboard`, `/properties/$propertyId` and `/properties/$propertyId/insights`
at 1440 px and 390 px. Every PR description cites a row number here. Nothing in
this table is re-litigated in review; a disagreement amends the row first.

## Why

The survey scored the two dashboard surfaces 19/40 against Nielsen's heuristics
and failed 7 of 8 cognitive-load checks. The findings were structural, not
cosmetic:

- The property Overview is 4,161 px at 1440 px (6.9 screens on a phone), carries
  three uncoordinated time windows, and one section honours none of them.
- Overview and Insights overlap ~50 %, in different vocabularies: `Amenities ·
Negative / Impact` on one page, `Amenities [Complaints] / Weighted impact` on
  the other.
- Provenance outranks the numbers. Every KPI printed `Ready · Data through Sep
11, 2026, 3:59 PM UTC`; the Google block printed `Current · Source · Retrieved
· Timezone · Period · Google data lag · Available until` above the fold.
- Charts were sized by their container, not their data: a 1088 × 612 px rating
  histogram with a hidden Y axis and no counts; ten years of daily buckets in
  1088 px with no year on the axis.
- Four of the first six numbers a manager sees were `—`.

## Decisions

| #   | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Dashboard is a section: **Overview** + three topic pages — **Ratings · Google · Guest voice**. Reply performance lives on Ratings as a "Responding" block. Portal analytics stay on the Portals page, which already has them.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 2   | Sidebar category following the shadcn `NavMain` pattern: `Dashboard` is a link to Overview with a `SidebarMenuAction` chevron toggling the sub-list; sub-items `Overview · Ratings · Google · Guest voice`; open when any dashboard route is active. `Insights` leaves the sidebar; `/insights` redirects to `/guests` permanently. Routes: `/properties/$propertyId`, `…/ratings`, `…/google`, `…/guests`.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 3   | `/dashboard` is deleted. `/properties` gains four comparison columns (rating · 30-day reviews · needs attention · portal). The org setup checklist becomes a one-line banner there, shown only while incomplete.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 4   | Overview = four rows, one laptop screen, **no charts**: **Attention** chips (or "Nothing needs your attention") → **Scorecard** of four tiles (Rating · Reviews · Reply rate · Profile views), each linking to its page → **Guest voice** headline sentence with top praise and complaint → **Recent reviews**, five rows, each opening the review.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| 5   | Overview has **no range control**. Tiles show identity (all-time) and pulse (last 30 days vs the prior 30) in fixed roles; one caption states the window once. Rating leads with all-time; Reviews and Profile views lead with the 30-day figure. The rating delta respects the existing ten-rating rule.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 6   | Topic pages share one range in `?range=`, carried across pages: **30 days · 90 days · 6 months · All time**, default **90 days**. Comparison is always the same length immediately before; All time has none. A source with shorter memory honours the range up to its limit and says so in one line — never with a second widget.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 7a  | **Ratings**: summary strip (avg rating + delta · reviews + delta · reply rate) → **rating over time** (bars = new reviews per bucket, line = **running average**) → **rating mix** as five horizontal bars with count and % → **Responding** (reply rate · reply time · awaiting a reply → inbox).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 7b  | **Google**: title "Google Business Profile", shared range, freshness mark + `Refresh` icon button → At a glance (Profile views · Website clicks · Call clicks · Direction requests; coverage line only when coverage is partial) → "How people found you" and "What people did" at 240 px, daily tables behind `<details>` → Additional interactions only when non-zero → methodology in an "About these numbers" disclosure. All eight error states and the unavailable/CTA state are unchanged.                                                                                                                |
| 7c  | **Guest voice**: headline sentence + supporting reviews + one basis line with the breakdown in a disclosure (**one** counter — fixes the notice/basis contradiction) → **Topics**, one row per topic: Praise · Complaints (each linking into the inbox) · Impact (diverging bar, one decimal) · Change vs prior → **Emerging issues** with change. The weekly-trend, sentiment and rating-distribution charts are removed from this page.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 8   | Availability renders **by exception**: nothing when a metric is ready; a state line with its reason only when it is not. Freshness marks appear only where lag is a real phenomenon — inline on the Google page (`Updated 4 min ago · Google data through Sep 10`), carried by the "still being analysed" line on Guest voice, absent on Ratings and Overview. Source detail lives in a popover on the Google page only. ADR 0007 gets a five-line note recording this reading.                                                                                                                                  |
| 9   | Charts: **two heights** via a `ChartFrame` wrapper (`compact` 160 px, `standard` 240 px; `aspect-video` forbidden). Buckets follow the range (30 d → day, 90 d and 6 mo → week, All time → month; ≤ 8 axis labels; the year on the first tick of each year). Fewer than 3 non-empty buckets → no chart, print the figures as a sentence. Every chart carries a computed one-line caption above it that doubles as its screen-reader description. Distributions are horizontal bars with count and %. Raw values live behind `<details>`. Legend only with more than one series. At most 2 charts above the fold. |
| 10  | Colour: **purple is interactive only** (links, active preset, selected rows — never a bar). Volumes in neutral ink. Green and red carry praise/complaints and up/down and **never appear without a sign, arrow, or label**. Red keeps its second job for "needs attention". Stars stay amber. Google's categorical series must stay distinguishable in grayscale.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| 11  | Vocabulary: Topic(s) · Impact (`+4.4`, one decimal, no version id) · Praise / Complaints (everywhere) · Profile views · Reviews without text · ratings (no "eligible") · Not analysed for topics · In an unsupported language · Portal scans / Private feedback · Average or Typical reply time, whichever matches the maths · Updated · AI analysis. An eight-term glossary (Impact · Praise/Complaints · Topics · Emerging issues · Needs attention · Reply rate · Profile views · Updated) lives in one module and is defined on first use per page.                                                          |
| 12  | Thin data: **a number where there is one, otherwise one sentence and one action — never a dash.** Each Overview element has three explicit states (has data · needs setup · filling in) pinned by a "week one" story. The AI sections never render nothing.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 13  | One information architecture for all screens. Overview fits **≤ 2 phone screens** (four tiles as 2 × 2); topic pages stack; tables become the stacked-list pattern; daily tables scroll inside their card; every target ≥ 44 px; 390 px stories are part of the gate.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 14  | **Structure only** — same tokens, type, radii, surfaces. Real `h2`s (18 px semibold, sentence case) replace the 14 px uppercase eyebrows; cards only where an edge groups something; sections 24 px apart, grids 16 px; the dashboard width tier drops from 1600 px to **1200 px**. A look refresh is a separate phase.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 15  | Seven PRs in dependency order — IA · Overview · Guest voice · Ratings · Google · Words & colour · Fleet → `/properties` — each auto-merging on green. PRs 3–5 may run in parallel after 1–2.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

## Definition of done, per PR

- Storybook stories for each touched page across {ready · week one · filling in
  · unavailable} × {1440 · 390}; `pnpm test:storybook` green (the
  `@storybook/addon-a11y` axe gate is the accessibility gate).
- `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, and the unit suites for the touched contexts.
- A before/after screenshot set from the live local stack on **Hotel Elegance**
  (259 reviews, AI on) and **Urban Move** (2 reviews, AI off) — the week-one
  case. The screenshots are the review; the diff is not.

## Non-goals

- No new read models, metrics, or backend contracts. The one bounded backend
  question this phase raises — whether the Google performance contract's `180d`
  maximum should grow so "All time" means more than six months there — is
  recorded and deferred; the page states its limit in a line either way.
- No visual refresh (row 14), no onboarding flows, no per-manager reply
  analytics (the reason Responding is a block and not a page).
