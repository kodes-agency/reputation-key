## Product-owner follow-up — 2026-09-14

The implemented result was corrected after review of the finished v2.1 work:

- the detail payload's rating is rendered immediately after the reviewer name;
- Inbox reads the current eligible Google reply observation, so a case closed
  by a reply already on Google also displays that reply's text;
- reply language remains inside the AI and template menus and is no longer
  repeated as a standalone control beside them;
- `Friendlier` and `Try again` are restored as one-click actions after an AI
  draft is adopted or reloaded; and
- on a phone, a read-only Google reply removes the unavailable Public reply
  mode, leaving one unambiguous `Add a note…` composer control.

These decisions supersede the older row 17, row 18, and row 20 amendments where
they describe the ghost language control, removal of the quick AI actions, or
the conflicting read-only phone segment.

## What the code says about the language

- The picker chooses between exactly two targets, _property default_ and
  _review language_ (`reply-language-options.ts`, `ReplyLanguageTarget`).
- Templates are per-language: `reply-template-operations.ts:137` filters the
  library by the target's `templateGroup`, and loading one stamps
  `replyLanguageTag` on the draft (`:304`). AI drafting does the same
  (`ai-suggested-draft-store.ts:360`).
- A hand-typed reply only records the tag (`reply-operations.ts:325`);
  nothing reads it at publish and Google accepts any language.

So the language is a parameter of the two assist actions, not of the reply.
Amended 2026-09-13 (PR 5 housekeeping): true of the server, not of the client.
The composer will not autosave or submit while the language is `Detect
automatically` (`use-reply-composer.ts:248`, `:266-270`); row 17's PR 4
amendment records what that changed.
