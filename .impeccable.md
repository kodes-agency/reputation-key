## Design Context

### Users

Organization administrators and property managers responsible for multi-location reputation operations. They work primarily at a desk but must complete urgent connection, import, and recovery tasks on a phone. Their job is to connect trusted sources, understand current state, act without ambiguity, and recover safely from partial failures.

### Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. Reputation Key should feel like a dependable operating console: human-readable and approachable, but never casual about authorization, source provenance, or destructive actions.

### Aesthetic Direction

An understated, information-led product UI built on the existing Satoshi body type, Plus Jakarta Sans display type, semantic purple accent, compact radii, restrained borders, and light/dark token system. Prefer clear editorial hierarchy and strong whitespace over decorative surfaces. Avoid generic card piles, provider-brand mimicry, raw technical identifiers, and color-only status communication.

### Design Principles

1. Make current state, source, freshness, and next action explicit before adding visual flourish.
2. Preserve one coherent hierarchy from 320 px through wide desktop; core actions remain reachable with 44 px touch targets and no hover dependency.
3. Use existing shadcn primitives and semantic tokens; custom composition may improve hierarchy but must not invent parallel interaction semantics.
4. Treat loading, expiry, revocation, partial failure, and recovery as first-class product states rather than exceptional footnotes.
5. Keep provider content ephemeral and visibly distinct from confirmed tenant input; render all external strings as plain escaped text.
