# Review analysis v3 test set

The synthetic core of the evaluation set for review analysis v3 (contract section 8,
decisions 5 and 6 of `v3-contract-decisions.md`). Every review here is invented: invented
names, places and businesses, written by two model families to a quota
(`testset-quota.md`), then labelled by both families under the annotation guide v3 with its
clarifications v3.1, v3.2 and v3.3. No real guest review, and no text from a real business,
belongs in this folder. The live slice that measures the beta organizations' own reviews is a
separate table keyed by review ID that holds no text (ADR 0031, amendment "Guest voice 2.0").

| File                 | What it holds                                                               |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `items-codex.jsonl`  | 200 reviews generated by Codex (`cx-0001` to `cx-0200`)                     |
| `items-claude.jsonl` | 200 reviews generated by Claude (`cl-0001` to `cl-0200`)                    |
| `items-urgent.jsonl` | 24 Bulgarian and Spanish Urgent and near-miss reviews (`cu-0001` to `0024`) |
| `gold-v1.1.jsonl`    | the settled labels of 419 of the 424 reviews, in the v3 answer's shape      |

An item is `{ id, business, rating, language, text, sentences, tags }`. `business` is a Google
primary category, `language` is `en`, `bg`, `es`, `de` or `mixed`, `sentences` is the text cut by
the production splitter (`src/shared/review-sentences.ts`), and `tags` record what the item was
written to test (urgent cases, near misses, names, sarcasm, rating conflicts, content-free
text). A gold label is `{ id, sentiment, mentions, urgent, gold_source }`; `gold_source` says
which round settled it, and a `+v1.1` suffix says the label was reworded for gold v1.1.

Five items have no gold label yet: `cl-0021`, `cl-0054`, `cl-0163`, `cl-0174` and `cl-0188`. The
two families still disagreed after three rounds and the owner settles them. The harness leaves
them out of every score and names them in its report.

## Gold v1.1 and guide v3.3 (2026-10-30)

The first OpenAI quality check read thing agreement at 0.51 against gold v1. The largest single
cause was not the provider's: in 96 of the 412 disagreeing pairs the labellers had written a
generic word as the `thing` of a mention (`place`, `staff`, `team`, `welcome`, `price`...) and the
provider had left it null, which is what the guide's own rule for a `thing` asks ("a specific
object the guest names, null otherwise"). Guide v3.3 states that rule, with the rest of what the
check found, and gold v1.1 applies it:

- 275 generic `thing` values became null in 167 labels (the issue stays). The words are the
  list in `src/shared/review-generic-things.ts`, the same list the prompt gives the model. The
  loader refuses any gold label that names one, so the rule cannot drift back in. Four mentions
  that became identical (same topic, null thing, same polarity) were merged the way the
  production normalizer merges them.
- Three causes the stored contract could not hold as written were reworded: `check-in: 30
minutes` (cl-0090), `wait: 40 minutes` (cl-0179) and a cause that repeated the review
  (cl-0147). The harness now finds no gold label its normalizer would change.
- 24 reviews were added to measure Urgent in Bulgarian and Spanish, which the set could not
  measure before: 14 true Urgent cases (6 safety, 4 health, 2 discrimination, 2 legal threat,
  seven in each language) and 10 near misses that look similar and are not (a billing dispute, a
  hazard fixed during the stay, a hypothetical risk, pests with no harm, a short safety briefing
  or too little supervision). Codex wrote the texts; Claude and Codex then labelled each one
  without seeing the other's labels. They agreed on Urgent in all 24, on sentiment in 23 and on
  the topics of 14. The ten items they disagreed on were settled by the guide, and the
  `gold_source` of those ten says so (`urgent_resolved_by_guide_v3.3`).

Thing agreement between the two families themselves, by the harness's own metric over the 400
first-round labels (1,089 paired mentions), is 0.59, and 0.66 once generic words are null on
both sides. The release gate for thing agreement is therefore 0.60
(`scripts/evaluation/review-analysis-v3-gates.ts` gives the reasons).

There are 44 Urgent cases now: 30 in English (11 safety, 9 health, 4 discrimination, 6 legal
threat) and 7 each in Bulgarian and Spanish (3 safety, 2 health, 1 discrimination, 1 legal
threat each). Urgent recall in all three languages can be measured.

Run the harness with `pnpm eval:review-analysis-v3` (see `scripts/evaluation/`). It never calls
a provider in tests or CI.
