# UI/UX — state of the app, 2026-09-02

Scope: `src/routes/**` (76 files), `src/components/**` (606 files; 453 production / 93 stories / 60 tests by my census), judged against `PRODUCT.md`, `DESIGN.md`, `.impeccable.md`, the three 2026-08-19 design documents, plan packages `EXP-01..03`, `MET-01`, and §3.6.2–3.6.3. Storybook was already running on `:6006`; four stories were rendered read-only (dark mode, 1280px and 360px) to ground visual claims. No dev server was started; nothing was edited outside this file.

## Verdict

The single most consequential fact: the flagship manager surface, `/properties/$propertyId` ("Overview"), contradicts the §3.6 metrics contract in four independently verifiable ways — it defaults to **All Time** instead of rolling 30 days (`src/routes/_authenticated/properties/$propertyId/index.tsx:18`, `src/contexts/dashboard/application/dto/dashboard.dto.ts:23`), it renders the Google average-rating delta as a **percentage** (`src/contexts/dashboard/infrastructure/repositories/dashboard.repository.ts:98-104` → `src/components/features/property/property-dashboard-helpers.tsx:9-12`), it coerces an empty period to **"0.0"** for Avg Rating and **"0%"** for reply rate (rendered in the `EmptyDashboard` story), and it carries **four incoherent period models** on one screen (page pills defaulting to All Time, a Google-performance range that says in its own copy "This range is independent from the Dashboard range above", a fixed 30-local-day AI window, and the AI trend's own window). The 2026-08-19 diagnosis — "the product computes far more than it shows" — is roughly half-cured: `ratingTrend`/`reviewVolume` are drawn, the 5-way attention breakdown is on both dashboards with deep links, `ai_property_daily_aggregates` category/sentiment counts are surfaced, the dead `InboxFilters` became a live filter popover, the `/metrics` placeholder and the "coming soon" notifications card are gone, "Edit & Resubmit" works. But of the proposal's three named dark sets only one is lit: the three `rollup_*` tables are still written by three `registration: 'enabled'` jobs with zero readers (`src/shared/governance/event-job-catalogue.ts:2763-2798`), the 15-column reply AI provenance still never reaches a read-only reply view (`src/components/inbox/reply-editor-views.tsx:23-27`), and beyond them AI quota/activity has no UI at all, trend history is fetched (32 rows) and one is shown, and Handling Cycles reach the client only as a concurrency token. The settings IA is still one flat nine-item list (`src/components/layout/settings-sidebar.tsx:42-111`) with property-scoped configuration now split across _three_ locations, and `/settings/organization` exposes two competing response timers ("Response SLA" drives the dashboard chip, "Response Target" drives the Inbox) in direct tension with §3.4.9. Brand fidelity is weak where it matters: every section of the flagship page wears an uppercase tracked eyebrow (28 occurrences / 16 files, contra `DESIGN.md` §6), five separate "big number + small label" card components exist (contra `PRODUCT.md` anti-references), and the `--success`/`--destructive-muted` tokens are defined but never exposed to Tailwind or used — all green/amber semantics are hardcoded palette classes (25 occurrences / 9 files) including two that fail AA on the dark theme. The component library itself is healthy and mostly live (3 dead files of 451; only `copy-button.tsx` is a real corpse), the Inbox is the strongest surface (real mobile layout, keyboard shortcuts, honest reply states, specific unavailability copy), and Portal analytics is the one place the §3.6.3 availability vocabulary is implemented completely.

## What was achieved

- **A1 — Reputation trend and volume are drawn.** `ratingTrend` + `reviewVolume` merged on date and rendered with the shadcn chart primitive; empty and sparse cases have copy and stories (`property-reputation-trend-chart.tsx`, stories `ReputationTrend*`).
- **A2 — Attention is a breakdown, not a number.** Property band (`attention-band.tsx`) and fleet badges (`fleet-attention-breakdown.tsx`) both expose all five signals; the property band deep-links each to a pre-filtered surface.
- **A3 — Guest topics from the AI aggregate table.** Category volume list with inbox click-through and a per-day sentiment stack, read from `ai_property_daily_aggregates` through a gated use case (`read-property-aggregates.ts`); "preparing" state explained rather than zeroed.
- **A4 — Dead surfaces removed:** `/metrics` placeholder (commit `92380205`), the `InboxFilters` corpse (now a real popover), the preferences "coming soon" card, billing UI, the org switcher.
- **A5 — Reply state machine is honest.** Eight views (`reply-status-view.tsx:16-37`), rejected → editable draft with AI controls, published → inline edit-and-republish, approved → "Waiting for Google", failed → "Check and retry" with the reconciliation rule stated in copy.
- **A6 — Setup checklist matches EXP-01** step for step, including degraded-with-history and PropertyManager waiting/no-access states.
- **A7 — Portal analytics implements §3.6.3 completely** (`Data through…`, `Updating`, `Insufficient data`, `Temporarily unavailable`, lifetime basis copy, availability-reason detail).

## What is genuinely good

- **G1 — The Inbox is the load-bearing surface and it holds.** Three-panel resizable desktop layout with a real mobile alternative (list, drawer, sheet), j/k/arrow/Escape shortcuts (`use-inbox-keyboard-shortcuts.ts`), skeleton/error/empty states (`inbox-list-states.tsx`), response-target card with overdue/completed/excluded presentation, bulk Reopen present and bulk Close absent exactly as §3.4.12 requires, and an AI suggestion flow that never injects into the draft, self-expires, and states "Nothing is published automatically" in the interface. Protect this; it is where the one tenant spends its time.
- **G2 — Availability copy is specific where it exists.** Nine distinct AI-unavailable reasons (`reply-suggestion-contract.ts`), seven portal availability reasons (`portal-metric-evidence-presentation.ts:43-63`), a local-fallback draft that is labelled as local. This is the "cannot vs failed" distinction the proposal asked for.
- **G3 — Storybook is the state matrix.** 93 story files, several with `play` assertions on states that matter (watermark preservation, email-capability denial, mandatory categories, narrow-name reflow). One design-QA pass with three iterations (`design-qa.md`) was done against rendered pixels, not prose.
- **G4 — Failure isolation on the dashboard.** AI trend and guest-topics sections self-fetch with `retry: false` and fail in place ("The rest of the dashboard is unaffected"), so a capability outage cannot take the page down (`property-ai-aggregate-section.tsx:24-30`).
- **G5 — Guest portal a11y basics are right.** Rating input is a real radio group with visible focus and 44px+ cells, honeypot is hidden from AT, `main` landmark, `lang` attribute, luminance-derived foreground on tenant colors (`guest-response-fields.tsx`, `portal-theme-style.ts`).
- **G6 — Reduced motion is global and correct** (`styles.css:280-288`), which is the only way to cover tw-animate-css.

## What should be improved

### UX-I01 Property KPI availability model is a three-state subset with coerced zeros

Evidence: `src/contexts/dashboard/domain/types.ts:14-50` (`KPIValue` has no evidence; `MetricKPIDataState = 'available'|'updating'|'unavailable'`); `property-dashboard-helpers.tsx:53-67`; `EmptyDashboard` story renders "AVG RATING 0.0 —" and "0% Reply Rate" (screenshot taken 2026-09-02).
Why it matters: §3.6.3 forbids coerced zero; a new tenant's first view says their rating is 0.0.
Recommendation: give `avgRating` and `reviews` the same evidence envelope as `scans`/`feedback`; render `—` with "No eligible reviews in this period"; add `insufficient_data` to `MetricKPIDataState` and route all four cards through `portalMetricEvidenceLine`.
Cost/risk: ~150 LOC across DTO/repo/helpers; existing helper tests already pin the "no `>0<`" contract.

### UX-I02 Avg Rating delta is a percent on the property dashboard

Evidence: `dashboard.repository.ts:98-104`; `property-dashboard-helpers.tsx:9-12,85-90`; visible "4.3 ↑ 4.9%".
Why: MET-01.5 says absolute stars with a 10-rating floor; fleet already does it (`fleet-overview.tsx:39-40`).
Recommendation: replace `trend` on `avgRating` with `comparison` (stars) + sample floor; reuse `formatRatingComparison`.

## What needs substantial change

### UX-S01 The dashboard's period model is wrong-shaped and contradicts §3.6.2

Evidence: default `'all'` (`properties/$propertyId/index.tsx:18`, `dashboard.dto.ts:23`); fleet `'30d'` hardcoded (`dashboard.tsx:40`); four independent windows on one page — page pills (`property-dashboard.tsx:80-104`), Google performance select+buttons with copy "This range is independent from the Dashboard range above" (`google-performance-section.tsx:63-105`), AI aggregates fixed 30 local days (`property-aggregates.ts:14-21`), AI trend window (`property-ai-trend-states.tsx:154-161`); measured: page pills 29px / performance buttons 44px, "7 Days" vs "7 days".

## Opinion

- The Overview page would read as a precision instrument if it were one band of definitions (`dl`), one chart, one list, with one period — the data is right, the chrome is what fights `PRODUCT.md`.
- I would not build the "Why this suggestion" disclosure or thumbs feedback until someone commits to reading the disposition column; the specific-copy work already bought most of the trust.
- The Inbox's "Categories" naming collision is small but it is the kind of thing a one-tenant beta _will_ report; fix vocabulary before layout.
- `neverthrow`-style `Result` in dashboard DTO validators (`toDashboardReplyStatus`) is fine; leave it.
